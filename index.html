📅 The Wednesweek CalendarWhat if we just deleted Wednesday? Six-day weeks, identical 30-day months and a four-day working week. Idea
Every month 30 days
Work dayWeekend
Wednesweek end of year

Five bonus holiday days after December for Christmas and New Year, plus a 6th in leap years. New Year's Day is the 5th of Wednesweek.

20 fewer working days a year, which is about 7.7% less work and 125 days off instead of 105.

  • 4-day work week. Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, then the weekend. No more hump day.
  • Every month is the same. 5 weeks × 6 days = 30 days, with no more random short months.
  • Dates never move. The 1st is always a Monday and every multiple of 6 is a Sunday, so planning is easy.
  • Less work. Work days drop from 71.4% of the week to 66.7%, which works out at 240 working days a year instead of 260.
  • Still adds up. 12 months × 30 days = 360, plus Wednesweek = 365. Halloween moves to 30 October.

“Thirty days has January, February, March… and all the rest, except for Wednesweek alone.”
